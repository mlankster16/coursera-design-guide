Your CTL project team

Most projects are supported by a standard team from CTL and Academic Media Services. The design team builds each Course from the Faculty Lead’s materials and manages the Coursera launch process. Your design team will vary based on project needs and scope. 

Project Manager / Senior Learning Experience Designer

Coordinates project activities, establishes internal timelines, and provides high-level learning experience design support across all Courses.

Learning Experience Designer

Guides pedagogy, copyright, accessibility, Course structure, and the overall learner experience for each Course.

Media Designer

Guides the visual design of videos and other Course elements, including graphics and visual treatments.

Media Producer, Academic Media Services

Supports studio recording and manages video production logistics, including shoot planning, recording coordination, and post-production editing.

How we share the work

You bring the subject-matter expertise and remain the primary content author. The CTL Design Team helps shape the learning experience and prepares it for Coursera.

As faculty, you will typically

  • Define and confirm the purpose of the Course or Specialization
  • Identify the learners you hope to reach
  • Confirm what learners should be able to do by the end
  • Provide or develop the content
  • Prepare scripts, slides, readings, activities, assessments, and projects
  • Review feedback and make content decisions
  • Confirm that the final experience is accurate and aligned with your goals
  • Review and approve the experience before it is submitted to Coursera

The CTL Design Team will help you

  • Translate your teaching expertise into an online learning experience
  • Offer feedback on the Specialization, Course, Module, and Learning Asset structure
  • Support decisions about sequencing and learner progression
  • Provide guidance on learning design, accessibility, and copyright
  • Review and refine content as it is developed
  • Build and QA the experience in Coursera
  • Monitor learner feedback and support needs after launch
