The destination

The goal

By the end of the Specialization, learners will be able to frame a business question, analyze and visualize relevant data, and communicate an evidence-informed recommendation.

Who this is for

Professionals who use data or make decisions informed by data but may not have formal training in analytics. They want to become more confident asking useful questions, interpreting evidence, and recommending a course of action.

Shaping the journey across Courses

Rather than dividing data analytics content into three roughly equal groups of topics, give each Course a distinct role in helping learners move toward the Specialization goal.

1 · Frame and prepare
2 · Analyze and visualize
3 · Recommend and communicate

Planning your own Specialization

Start with the learner’s need, then give each Course a clear role

  1. Begin with the goal

    It often helps to describe what learners will be able to do by the end, rather than the topics they’ll cover.

  2. Name what each Course leaves learners with

    Ideally, each Course leaves learners with something the next one can build on.

  3. Look at the progression

    Reading the outcomes in order is a quick way to see whether they add up to the goal.
