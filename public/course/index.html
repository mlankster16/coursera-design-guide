The destination

Course objectives

By the end of the Course, learners will be able to:

  1. 1Translate a business need into a focused question that can be investigated with data.
  2. 2Identify the data needed to investigate a business question.
  3. 3Evaluate a dataset for relevance, completeness, and basic quality concerns.
  4. 4Prepare a simple dataset for an introductory business analysis.

This Course’s role in the Specialization

Learners begin by framing a business question and considering what data they would need to investigate it. They then identify and evaluate available data before preparing a simple dataset for analysis.

Learners leave with

A focused business question and a simple, analysis-ready dataset

Grouping related learning into Modules

Looking across the planning notes for Foundations of Data Analytics for Business, related ideas could come together as three Modules.

Objectives and Modules don’t need to match one-to-one. Objectives 2 and 3 are developed across more than one Module, and each Module brings together goals from several objectives.

Before grouping: what each objective will require

For each objective, note what learners need, what you already have, and where there may be gaps. You don’t need to choose formats yet. One example:

Objective 3Evaluate a dataset for relevance, completeness, and basic quality concerns.

What learners need

Understand what makes data relevant to a particular question; recognize missing variables and common quality problems; distinguish issues that could affect an analysis from those that may be less consequential; explain how limitations could affect a conclusion.

What I already have

Customer dataset, a data-quality lecture designed for graduate students, a draft checklist, and a draft data dictionary.

What I may need

Clearer explanations of relevance, completeness, and suitability; examples from more than one industry; simplified versions of the lecture and checklist.

Questions

The customer database may be proprietary. Can we adapt it for Coursera, or do we need an open dataset? Would Coursera Labs support this kind of data review?

Real-world application

I have a realistic workplace dataset that might be useful for a hands-on activity. I’m imagining that learners could use it to move from a business need to a focused question, assess whether the data is suitable for that question, and prepare it for an introductory analysis. A short business brief, data-quality checklist, and guiding questions could support their decisions. The activity could be completed in a spreadsheet, without requiring specialized or paid software.

Planning your own Course

Start with the objectives, then group what learners need into Modules

  1. Draft 2–4 objectives

    Aim for each to describe something learners can demonstrate. Starting with an action verb helps.

  2. Think through what each one requires

    Jot down learner needs, materials you already have, possible gaps, and questions. Formats can come later.

  3. Group related learning

    Bring related goals together into Modules, checking that each objective appears in at least one.

Next, choose the Learning Assets for each Module.
